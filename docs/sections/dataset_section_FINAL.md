# Final Dataset & Sourcing Section

The solar flare forecasting and nowcasting model leverages primary observations from India's Aditya-L1 solar observatory, specifically integrating data from the Solar Low Energy X-ray Spectrometer (SoLEXS) and the High Energy L1 Orbiting X-ray Spectrometer (HEL1OS). SoLEXS provides the soft X-ray coronal emission baseline at 2–22 keV, while HEL1OS captures the high-energy, non-thermal hard X-ray impulses at 10–150 keV. This dual-channel configuration is cross-validated against the long-term historical archives of the NOAA GOES-15 and GOES-16 X-ray Sensors (XRS), which supply the historical training baseline.

Currently, data access is confirmed and operational for Level-1 scientific products via the ISRO Science Data Centre (ISSDC) PRADAN portal. Standardized files for SoLEXS (`.lc.gz`) and HEL1OS (`lightcurve_*.fits`) have been successfully retrieved, parsed, and merged. However, programmatic API integration remains pending; manual bulk downloads via session-based scripts are currently used.

The v1 merged dataset covers a ~12-hour UTC window of June 20, 2026 (specifically from 12:10:28 to 23:59:26 UTC), containing exactly 42,539 overlapping records at a synchronized 1-second cadence. This date represents the first simultaneous solar observation window where both payloads recorded active telemetry in June 2026. Preprocessing resamples the raw count rates to 1-second bins, rounding HEL1OS timestamps to the nearest integer second. An inner join filters the data strictly to the overlapping observation window. Background subtraction is performed by applying a rolling 10th percentile filter over a 42,539-point window to isolate active flare impulses from the quiet-Sun coronal background.

Known limitations include periodic data gaps due to orbital night-side eclipses for SoLEXS, telemetry dropouts, and instrument calibration offsets. Furthermore, GOES features a 1-minute averaged cadence compared to the native 1-second resolution of Aditya-L1, requiring downsampling during historical validation.
